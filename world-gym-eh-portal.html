The partnership

Why World Gym partnered with Employment Hero

Managing people in a fitness franchise is genuinely complex. Casual workforces, high turnover, shift-based rosters, Award compliance, and the pressure of running a business where your staff are your product — it demands more than generic HR software.

World Gym Australia selected Employment Hero as the network's designated HR and payroll platform because it covers the full employment lifecycle in a single system — from the moment you post a job through to processing payroll and managing performance — with the compliance infrastructure Australian franchises need built in by default.

Network consistency

Every World Gym franchise runs on the same platform with the same structure. That means consistent onboarding, consistent compliance practices, and consistent payroll treatment — regardless of location or ownership.

Built for Australian compliance

Employment Hero is built for the Australian employment market — Fair Work, STP Phase 2, SuperStream, and Award interpretation are not afterthoughts. They're core to how the platform works.

One system, not three

HR, payroll, and time and attendance are integrated in a single platform. Leave approvals flow into payroll. Timesheet data flows into pay runs. New hires flow from onboarding into the employee record — no re-keying, no reconciliation.

Scales with your franchise

Whether you operate one location or multiple, the platform scales without re-implementation. Multi-site visibility, consolidated reporting, and a single employee app across your entire business.
